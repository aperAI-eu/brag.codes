# brag.codes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026.

## Who we are

brag.codes is operated by aperAI OÜ (Estonian registry code 17446295),
Narva mnt 5, 10117 Tallinn, Estonia. We are responsible for the personal data
processed to run this service. For privacy requests, contact **hello@aperai.eu**.

## Information we process and why

brag.codes lets you describe projects and choose to share them publicly. We process:

- **Account information:** your chosen Google or GitHub account identifier, name, email address,
  verification status and profile image when supplied, together with authentication
  and session records. These let you sign in and control your projects. We do not
  receive your provider password. GitHub sign-in, when enabled, reads basic identity
  and verified email information, including private email addresses. It requests no
  repository permissions, and provider access tokens are not retained.
- **Your contributions:** project descriptions, event associations, submitted agent
  proposals, and any links or contact details you provide. These let us save, edit
  and publish the content you choose.
- **Moderation records:** when platform administrators hide or restore a published project,
  we record the action, reason, administrator identity/display name and time. These records
  are visible only to platform administrators and are removed when the project is permanently
  deleted.
- **Optional voice pitches:** recordings, transcripts and generated descriptions,
  when you use voice capture. Review generated text before saving or publishing it.
- **Technical and support information:** connection and security records, which may
  include IP addresses, browser details and request times, and messages you send us.
  These help us operate the service, prevent abuse and respond to requests.

We use account and contribution information as needed to provide the service you
request (performance of a contract). Security and troubleshooting rely on our
legitimate interests in protecting the service and its users. We process information
when needed to comply with legal obligations. If a separate activity requires
consent, we ask for it separately; you may withdraw it without affecting earlier
lawful processing.

You can browse public content without an account. Signing in requires account
information; recording a pitch and supplying public contact details are optional.
We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

## What becomes public and who receives data

Publishing makes your approved project information, including any contact details
you include, available to anyone. Others may copy or index it. Private drafts,
recordings, transcripts and sign-in credentials are not part of that publication.
Do not submit passwords, secrets or personal information you are not entitled to share.

Authorized service personnel and hosting and backup providers may process private
information where needed to operate, secure or support brag.codes. Google handles sign-in under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
GitHub handles its sign-in under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
When you choose voice processing, aperAI Distill receives the recording for
transcription and Anthropic receives the transcript to prepare a project description.
Their infrastructure and any subprocessors may also process that information.
We may disclose specific information where legally required or necessary for legal claims.

For information about processing locations and applicable safeguards for transfers
outside the EEA, including how to obtain a copy of relevant safeguards, contact
hello@aperai.eu.

## Cookies and retention

We use cookies needed for sign-in and session security. The application does not
include advertising or analytics trackers.

Account and saved project information is retained while needed to provide your
account and projects. You can permanently delete your own project from My projects
using Delete project. This removes its saved content and public page, cancels pending
processing and revokes its project-specific agent access. Cleanup of processor copies
can take longer. We retain minimal deletion identifiers to prevent an older backup
from restoring deleted content. Contact us to request account deletion. Limited records may remain
where necessary for a legal obligation, an unresolved security incident or a legal
claim. Public copies held independently by others may remain after removal here.

Local pitch audio is scheduled for deletion after successful transcription,
cancellation or the 24-hour retention deadline. Transcript and proposal content
expires after 30 days; text you accept into a saved project follows that project's
lifecycle. Cleanup can take time to complete. Audio is excluded from our backups.
Deletion requests sent to processing providers do not guarantee immediate deletion
from their backups.

Sign-in sessions expire after seven days; expiry is not the same as deletion of
stored records. Security and support records are kept only while needed for the
incident, enquiry or applicable legal obligations. Restricted backup copies expire
through backup rotation.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction
or a portable copy of your personal data, and object to processing based on
legitimate interests. Email hello@aperai.eu; we may verify your identity before
acting. You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate or your local
data protection authority. Removing Google or GitHub authorization does not itself delete
your brag.codes account.

AI assists with drafting; it does not make decisions about you with legal or
similarly significant effects. We will update this policy when our practices
change and provide notice of material changes as required by law.
